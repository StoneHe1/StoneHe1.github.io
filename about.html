About

Stone He is a Chinese Canadian __ Toronto based bassist, keyboardist, composer, and arranger that has performed in a variety of genres including classical, jazz, pop, rock, and funk genres. He performs with various groups and orchestras across the Greater Toronto Area, including the Toronto Pops Orchestra, Rosedale Symphony Orchestra, Grange Park Jazz Club, Exit Points, and Orchestre Salon de Musique. In addition, He has performed with small chamber groups and led jazz bands across the Toronto music scene, as well as participating in recording projects on bass and keyboard for various groups.

In early 2024, He participated in the first Side-by-Side program organized by Confluence Concerts. In that program, He collaborated with other musicians of different musical backgrounds to plan a concert programme in a span of two weeks. His experience in the program was a critical point in his music journey to explore more in coming up with his own sound and combining musical genres together.

Currently, He is a bassist, arranger, and personnel manager of the Toronto Pops Orchestra. Aside from music, He enjoys a variety of interests including tennis, skiing, bouldering, traveling, language learning, and gliding - whenever He finds the time that is. Outside of music he also works as a Junior Accident Reconstructionist.
